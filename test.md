# Website Renewal & Maintenance Charges

**Project:** Website Development
**Initial Project Cost:** NPR 40,000

The initial project package of **NPR 40,000** includes website development along with **one year of domain registration, web hosting, and basic website maintenance**.

After the completion of the first year, the following annual charges will apply for continuing the website services:

| Service                                 | Annual Estimated Cost |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------: |
| Domain Renewal                          |             NPR 2,600 |
| Web Hosting Renewal                     |             NPR 3,500 |
| Website Maintenance & Technical Support |             NPR 2,000 |
| **Total Annual Renewal & Maintenance**  |        **NPR 8,100** |

### Maintenance Includes

* Website technical support
* Bug fixing related to the existing website
* Basic website monitoring
* Minor content updates
* Basic troubleshooting
* Assistance with hosting and domain-related issues

### Additional Charges

Any major changes, redesigns, new features, new pages, or additional functionality not included in the existing website scope will be quoted and charged separately based on the requirements.

### Renewal Terms

The annual renewal and maintenance charges will be applicable after the completion of the first year. Domain and hosting renewal costs may vary depending on the service provider and applicable renewal rates at the time of renewal.

**Note:** The client will be informed of the renewal charges before the renewal period, and the services will be continued upon confirmation.
